“The best way to predict your future is to create it.”

Hi, my name is Vivaan. I’m a Grade 12 student from Kolkata.

I’m interested in economics and finance, but not in a purely academic way. What interests me more is how things actually work outside the classroom. Why people make the decisions they do, why systems succeed or fail, and what separates something that looks good on paper from something that actually works in reality.

A lot of how I think comes from trying things out instead of overthinking them. I don’t really believe in waiting for the “right time.” Most of the time, clarity comes after action, not before it. I’ve learned more from things not going as planned than from things working perfectly.

I tend to look at things in terms of structure. Whether it’s a team, a project, or even personal habits, I’m always thinking about what’s underneath it. What makes it sustainable, what breaks under pressure, and how it can be improved. That’s something that naturally pulled me towards economics and finance, because both deal with systems that are constantly changing and rarely predictable.

At the same time, I don’t think everything is just about logic. People are unpredictable. Emotions, incentives, and pressure often matter more than theory. Understanding that balance between structure and human behavior is something I find really interesting.

Outside of this, I like things that slow me down a bit.

I enjoy watching movies, not just for entertainment but for the way they tell stories and build perspective. I listen to a lot of music, and I play the guitar as well. It’s one of the few things where I’m not trying to optimize anything, just enjoying the process.

I’ve also been involved in dramatics and acting, which taught me something completely different. It made me more aware of expression, presence, and how people perceive things. It’s very different from analytical thinking, but in a way, it complements it.

I’d describe myself as ambitious, but I don’t think of ambition as just achieving things. For me, it’s more about building a life that has direction and meaning. I don’t want to just do things for the sake of doing them. I want whatever I work on to matter, at least in some way.

I also believe in giving back. Not in a symbolic sense, but in a real, tangible way. If you’re building something, it should extend beyond just yourself. Otherwise, it starts to feel limited.

Right now, I’m exploring where economics, finance, and entrepreneurship meet, and how I can use that to build something meaningful over time.

I don’t have everything figured out, and I don’t think I’m supposed to at this stage. But I care about the direction I’m moving in.
