Mist Fragrance

A student-led fragrance venture focused on branding, consumer behavior, and building a product from idea to market.

The Problem

Premium fragrances are often expensive and inaccessible to younger consumers. At the same time, many student entrepreneurs struggle to gain real-world experience in product development, branding, and sales.

Mist was created to bridge that gap by offering affordable fragrances while building a real business from the ground up.

Why I Started Mist

Mist started on a hot summer afternoon in school.

A few hours after applying perfume in the morning, the fragrance had completely faded. Carrying a full-sized bottle throughout the day was inconvenient, yet most portable perfume atomizers available online were surprisingly expensive.

That raised a simple question:

Why wasn't there an affordable option designed specifically for students?

After researching suppliers and wholesalers, I sourced compact refillable perfume atomizers and launched Mist with the goal of making portable fragrances accessible at a student-friendly price point.

What began as a simple solution to an everyday problem evolved into a functioning consumer venture involving product sourcing, branding, sales, team management, and investor engagement.

What We Built

  • Consumer fragrance brand
  • Product sourcing and packaging
  • Brand identity and marketing strategy
  • Direct-to-consumer sales model
  • Exhibition and event-based distribution
  • Customer feedback system

Journey

Mist evolved through multiple iterations of branding, pricing, packaging, and customer acquisition.

Each sale provided insights into consumer behavior, demand patterns, pricing psychology, and product-market fit.
