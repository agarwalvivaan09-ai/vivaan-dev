INDUSTRIAL POLICY · TRADE · MANUFACTURING

India Pays China $99 Billion to Compete with China

Research Fellow · Nikore Associates · Young Economist Programme · 2026

Research on India's industrial policy, Production Linked Incentive schemes and the country's $99.2 billion trade deficit with China.

I examined India's dependence on Chinese components, connected trade data with domestic manufacturing capacity, and developed policy recommendations aimed at strengthening domestic production and reducing supply-chain dependence.
