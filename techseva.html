TechSeva

A grassroots e-waste collection initiative focused on responsible disposal and environmental awareness.

The Problem

E-waste is one of the fastest-growing environmental challenges, yet awareness around proper disposal remains extremely low at the household level.

Most individuals accumulate electronic waste without knowing how or where to dispose of it safely.

Why I Started TechSeva

The idea for TechSeva didn’t start as a project — it started with a drawer.

While cleaning my room, I opened a drawer filled with old cables, broken devices, and unused electronics that had been sitting there for years. It felt insignificant at first — just clutter.

When I asked if I could throw everything out, I was told that e-waste can be extremely harmful if not disposed of properly. That moment shifted my perspective.

If I had this much e-waste sitting unused, it was likely that many others did too. What looked like a small, personal problem was actually much larger.

That realization led to TechSeva — an initiative focused on collecting e-waste from local households and ensuring it was responsibly recycled.

What I Did

TechSeva was initiated as a focused effort to collect e-waste directly from local households, starting with family, friends, and immediate community networks.

The initiative involved outreach, awareness, and coordination with a certified recycling organization to ensure responsible disposal.
